👥 HR Workforce Attrition Dashboard


📌 Project Overview
The HR team was spending hours generating monthly attrition reports manually.
This dashboard automates that process and gives leadership instant visibility into
who is leaving, why, and which groups are most at risk.
Built in 2 iterations — one for 1,470 employees, one for 961 — to validate findings across different datasets.

🛠️ Tools Used
ToolPurposePower BIDashboard design, drill-through, slicersDAX16 custom KPI measuresAdvanced ExcelData cleaning, pre-processing


🔍 Key Findings

🔴 Age group 26–35 has the highest attrition rate — 16% (v1) and 14% (v2)
⚡ Built 16 DAX KPI measures that cut monthly report generation time by 80%
📉 Employees with less than 2 years tenure showed 3x higher attrition vs senior staff
🏢 Sales and R&D departments had the highest voluntary exit rates



💡 What I Learned

How to build a star schema data model in Power BI
Writing complex DAX measures for dynamic KPI calculation
Designing dashboards with drill-through by department, age, and gender
Translating raw HR data into business-ready insights
